Tickets

Frontier: takeable now

  • 01

    Destination & pilot department

    Grilling · HITL

    What exactly is v1 for? Confirm the destination (a locked v1 spec for one pilot department) and pin the pilot's profile: specialty, headcount, one vs many Departments, country/regulator, who the champion is, and the go-live month.

  • 02

    Confirm or overturn the provisional ADRs

    Grilling · HITL

    ADR-0001…0008 were decided by the agent under delegated ownership, without a grilling session. Walk each one with the owner: keep, amend, or supersede. Highest-stakes: modular monolith on Next.js/Postgres (0001), shared-DB tenancy (0003), auto-approved trades (0005), PWA instead of native (0007).

  • 03

    Where Zoe lives: repository, hosting, environments

    Grilling · HITL

    This repository was borrowed only for its instance; its original code is unrelated. Decide Zoe's permanent home (new repo? org?), hosting target (Vercel, Fly, AWS, Azure for hospital buyers), environments (dev/staging/prod), and CI.

  • 04

    Business model & licensing

    Grilling · HITL

    MIT open source, open-core with paid enterprise modules, or closed SaaS? Who buys (department chair, health-system IT, medical group)? Price shape (per provider/month, flat per department)? This decides what can stay MIT and what features are 'enterprise'.

  • 05

    Research: compliance for scheduling data (HIPAA, SOC 2, BAAs)

    Research · AFK

    Is a clinical work schedule PHI? When does a scheduling vendor need a BAA, SOC 2 Type II, HITRUST? What do hospital security questionnaires require (SSO, audit logs, data residency, encryption, pen tests)? Cite primary sources (HHS, AICPA, vendor trust pages).

  • 06

    Research: hospital identity providers & SSO

    Research · AFK

    Which IdPs do target hospitals use (Entra ID/Azure AD, Okta, Ping, Imprivata)? SAML vs OIDC prevalence, SCIM provisioning expectations, and whether Better Auth (or alternatives: WorkOS, Auth.js, Clerk) covers SAML+SCIM well enough for v1.

  • 07

    Research: Epic / paging / clinical-comms integration paths

    Research · AFK

    How do on-call tools feed Epic On-Call Finder and Secure Chat (interfaces, App Orchard/Showroom, HL7/FHIR, flat files)? Same for Spok, Vocera, PerfectServe, TigerConnect. Effort and certification cost for each.

  • 08

    Research: payroll & HCM export formats

    Research · AFK

    File/API formats and pay-code models for ADP, UKG/Kronos, Workday, Dayforce. What minimum export would let a pilot run payroll from Zoe Time Entries?

  • 09

    Research: constraint solvers for Auto-fill at scale

    Research · AFK

    Compare greedy (today) vs CP-SAT (OR-Tools), Timefold, and MILP for departments of 20–300 staff: solution quality on fairness, run time, explainability, licensing, and how to call them from Node (WASM, sidecar service).

Blocked

  • 10

    Task: collect a real (anonymised) schedule and rule book from the pilot

    Task · HITL

    Obtain 3 months of the pilot department's past schedule, their written rules (rest, call limits, fairness expectations, holiday rotation), and current request/swap volumes. Without this, rule and solver decisions are guesses.

    Blocked by 01 · Destination & pilot

  • 11

    Rule catalogue for v1

    Grilling · HITL

    Which Rule kinds must v1 express beyond today's (tags, overlap, time off, rest, consecutive days, weekly max, preferences)? Candidates: holiday rotation, weekend pairing, post-call day off, call-per-month caps, min/max per Shift Type, series rules, target percentages, seniority. Hard vs Soft defaults.

    Blocked by 01 · Destination & pilot, 10 · Real schedule sample

  • 12

    Prototype: how schedulers author Rules

    Prototype · HITL

    Plain-language rule builder vs form-per-rule vs templates per specialty. Prototype 2–3 variants with the pilot scheduler; the reported pain is 'needed vendor help to configure'.

    Blocked by 11 · Rule catalogue

  • 13

    Auto-fill strategy for v1

    Grilling · HITL

    Keep the deterministic greedy fill, add a solver adapter behind the same interface, or both (greedy for edits, solver for full builds)? Must keep: never move Locked Assignments, fill only gaps on request, explain every pick.

    Blocked by 09 · Solver options, 10 · Real schedule sample, 11 · Rule catalogue

  • 14

    Production auth, tenancy isolation and data residency

    Grilling · HITL

    Pick the auth provider and SSO/SCIM scope; confirm shared-DB + Postgres RLS (ADR-0003) or move to DB-per-tenant for enterprise buyers; region/data-residency commitments.

    Blocked by 05 · Compliance: HIPAA / SOC 2, 06 · Hospital SSO & IdPs, 03 · Where Zoe lives

  • 15

    Notification channels & urgency model

    Grilling · HITL

    Which channels in v1 (in-app, email, SMS, web push, native push)? What counts as urgent (uncovered on-call within 24h?) and who gets it? Quiet hours, digests, per-user tuning — the incumbent's duplicates/noise is a top complaint.

    Blocked by 01 · Destination & pilot, 04 · Business model & licensing

  • 16

    Prototype: mobile delivery — PWA vs native shell

    Prototype · HITL

    Can an installable PWA meet staff expectations (push on iOS, offline schedule, biometric unlock, never-logged-out)? Prototype the PWA on iOS/Android and a Capacitor shell; decide whether ADR-0007 stands.

    Blocked by 15 · Notification channels, 02 · Confirm provisional ADRs

  • 17

    Integration scope for v1

    Grilling · HITL

    Which of Epic On-Call Finder, paging vendors, payroll exports, calendar feeds, public REST API ship in v1 vs later — driven by what the pilot actually needs to switch.

    Blocked by 07 · Epic / paging paths, 08 · Payroll formats, 01 · Destination & pilot

  • 18

    Nurse & staff scheduling scope

    Grilling · HITL

    Is v1 provider-only (physicians, CRNAs, APPs) or also nursing units (patterns, self-scheduling windows, float pool, census/acuity, open-shift broadcast)? This roughly doubles the rule and UX surface.

    Blocked by 01 · Destination & pilot

  • 19

    Credentialing scope

    Grilling · HITL

    Tags with expiry dates that gate eligibility (light) vs full credentialing/privileging/payer enrollment (heavy, a product of its own). What does the pilot need on day one?

    Blocked by 01 · Destination & pilot

  • 20

    Migration & onboarding from the incumbent

    Grilling · HITL

    How does a department move in: import staff, Shift Types, history (for fairness carry-over) from the incumbent's API/exports or spreadsheets? Parallel-run period? Who does setup — self-serve wizard or white-glove?

    Blocked by 10 · Real schedule sample, 01 · Destination & pilot

  • 21

    Lock the v1 spec

    Grilling · HITL

    Fold every resolved decision into one v1 spec (via /to-spec), cut scope to what the pilot needs to go live, and hand it to /to-tickets. This ticket closing means the destination is reached.

  • Public API and webhooks. Waits on integration scope.
