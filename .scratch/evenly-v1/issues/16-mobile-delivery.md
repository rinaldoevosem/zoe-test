# 16: Prototype: mobile delivery — PWA vs native shell

Type: prototype
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 15, 02
Map: [Evenly v1 map](../map.md)

## Question

Can an installable PWA meet staff expectations (push on iOS, offline schedule, biometric unlock, never-logged-out)? Prototype the PWA on iOS/Android and a Capacitor shell; decide whether ADR-0007 stands.

## Answer

Owner's answers in the stakeholder questionnaire, 2026-11-16 (https://claude.ai/artifact/1se9TniXGgXXBxQGK8yCMb):

- **Beta: the installable web app** (home screen, no app store), as ADR-0007.
- **General availability requires App Store and Google Play apps** (native shells around the same app). ADR-0007 amended.
