# 18: Nurse & staff scheduling scope

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01
Map: [Evenly v1 map](../map.md)

## Question

Is v1 provider-only (physicians, CRNAs, APPs) or also nursing units (patterns, self-scheduling windows, float pool, census/acuity, open-shift broadcast)? This roughly doubles the rule and UX surface.

## Answer

Owner's answers in the stakeholder questionnaire, 2026-11-16 (https://claude.ai/artifact/1se9TniXGgXXBxQGK8yCMb):

- **Provider-only** for v1: physicians and advanced practice providers. Nursing units and residents later.
