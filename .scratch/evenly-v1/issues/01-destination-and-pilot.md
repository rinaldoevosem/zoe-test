Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: None (can start immediately)

## Question

What exactly is v1 for? Confirm the destination (a locked v1 spec for one pilot department) and pin the pilot's profile: specialty, headcount, one vs many Departments, country/regulator, who the champion is, and the go-live month.

## Answer

Owner's answers in the stakeholder questionnaire, 2026-11-16 (https://claude.ai/artifact/1se9TniXGgXXBxQGK8yCMb):

- v1 starts with **one Department** (pilot), then expands.
- Pilot specialty: **hospital medicine** (hospitalists; day teams, admitters, nocturnists, APPs), replacing the anesthesiology assumption.
- Go-live: **within three months** (target mid-February 2027) with a smaller first version.
- Scheduled population: physicians and advanced practice providers only. Residents and nursing units are out of scope for v1.
- The pilot will share three months of past schedules and its written rules (ticket 10).
