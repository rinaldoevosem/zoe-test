# 19: Credentialing scope

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01
Map: [Evenly v1 map](../map.md)

## Question

Tags with expiry dates that gate eligibility (light) vs full credentialing/privileging/payer enrollment (heavy, a product of its own). What does the pilot need on day one?

## Answer

Owner's answers in the stakeholder questionnaire, 2026-11-16 (https://claude.ai/artifact/1se9TniXGgXXBxQGK8yCMb):

- **Light**: Tags can carry an **expiry date**; an expired Tag no longer qualifies a Staff Member, and people are reminded before it expires.
- Full credentialing, privileging and payer enrolment stay out of scope.
